What this dashboard covers. Every healthcare job posting live on the Bundesagentur für Arbeit (BA) Jobbörse on 2026-11-20, taken from the BA occupational fields 811, 812, 813, 814, 817, 818, 821, 825 (KldB 2010). 92,321 postings in Germany are shown (3,127 postings located abroad were left out, because the StepStone dashboards cover Germany only). The layout, columns and charts are the same as the StepStone dashboard so the two can be read side by side.

Methods & Caveats
Source. BA Jobsuche interface (rest.arbeitsagentur.de, v6), collected in 50 query slices that add up to the full count for each occupational field. 95,448 of 98,759 listed postings were retrieved (96.6%). The remainder sit behind the interface's 10,000-result limit.
Occupation. Each posting carries the BA occupation (hauptberuf). It is matched to the official KldB 2010 title directory and converted to ISCO-08 with the BA crosswalk (primary link). No AI step is involved. ISCO_Source records the route: exact title match, normalised match, or the occupational field's most common code.
Employer sector. Agency flags from the BA (temporary staffing, private placement) come first, then employer-name rules, then the BA industry filter (branche), then the employer's other postings. Employer_Category_Confidence_Level drives the "Hide unclear categories" filter as in the StepStone dashboard.
Dates. Date_Posted is the first publication date. BA postings stay listed until filled, so dates reach back years. The weekly chart covers the last 26 weeks, and older postings still count in every total.
Job titles. German originals, cleaned in the browser like the StepStone titles. Full German text is kept in Title_DE and Description (sampled postings only).
Counts. Postings, not vacancies. The official BA vacancy stock (registered vacancies with a placement mandate) is shown alongside for the StepStone month, June 2026.
